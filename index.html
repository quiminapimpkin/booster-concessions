How the numbers work

  • Revenue = what customers paid, excluding voided sales. Combo discounts are already taken off, and each item’s share of the discount is taken off its own revenue and profit.
  • Cost of goods = unit cost × units sold, using the cost at the moment of the sale.
  • Gross profit = revenue − cost of goods. Margin = gross profit ÷ revenue.
  • Write-offs = waste, spoilage, giveaways and count shortages, valued at cost. Net profit = gross profit − write-offs.
  • Spent on stock = money paid when you log a restock. Cash result = revenue − spent on stock, which is what the club actually kept for the period.
  • When you restock, the unit cost becomes a weighted average of the old stock and the new purchase.

Game-day checklist

  1. Before: tap the event pill at the top and start today’s event. Log anything you bought under Inventory → Restock. On the Till tab, count your starting change by bill and coin and tap Open till.
  2. During: tap items on the Register. With 3+ items, tap Combo discount for $1 off (once per 3 items). Pick Cash / Card / Other, and Complete sale. Made a mistake? Undo last sale.
  3. After: on the Till tab, count the drawer and Close till to see over/short and what to deposit. Then do a quick count of what’s left. Use Inventory → Adjust → Count where the number differs. Then tap the event pill and End event.
